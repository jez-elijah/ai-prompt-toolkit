# Using AI Safely at Work

A short guide for colleagues who use AI tools but are not technical.

## What these tools are (and are not)

Tools like Claude or ChatGPT are **generative AI**. They predict useful text based on patterns learned from large amounts of writing. They are very good at drafting, summarizing, and explaining. They are **not** a search engine or a fact database, and they can state wrong things in a confident tone. Treat the output as a first draft from a fast assistant, not as a final answer.

## Five habits that keep you safe

**1. Don't paste what you wouldn't email to a stranger.**
Keep out customer names with contact details, ID or account numbers, passwords, health or salary information, and confidential company or client documents. If you need help with a document that contains these, replace them with placeholders like [CLIENT] or [ACCOUNT] first, or use only a tool your company has approved for that data.

**2. Use approved tools.**
Free consumer tools may keep or learn from what you type. Your company may have an approved tool with better privacy settings. Ask before using a new one.

**3. Check the facts.**
AI can invent numbers, quotes, names, and sources. Verify anything that matters: figures, policies, legal or medical statements, and anything you will send to a client.

**4. You are responsible for the result.**
If you send AI-written text under your name, it is yours. Read it fully, fix the tone, and remove anything you cannot stand behind.

**5. Be open about it.**
Follow your team's rules on disclosing AI use, especially for client work, reports, and anything official.

## Writing better prompts

A good prompt has four parts:

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| **Role** | "You are a friendly HR assistant." |
| **Task** | "Summarize these notes." |
| **Rules** | "Use only the notes. Say 'Unclear' if unsure. Under 100 words." |
| **Format** | "Give 3 bullets and a list of action items." |

Telling the tool what **not** to do (for example, "do not invent dates") works well. If the first result is off, reply with what to fix instead of starting over.

## Quick check before you press send

- Is there anything private or confidential in what I pasted?
- Is this an approved tool for this kind of information?
- Will I verify the facts before using the answer?

If any answer is "I'm not sure", stop and ask your manager or the person responsible for AI rules in your team.

## Where to find help

Ask your team lead or the AI/IT contact in your organization. Reusable prompt templates are in the `templates/` folder of this toolkit.

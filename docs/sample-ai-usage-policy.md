# Sample AI Usage Policy (Draft)

> **Sample for learning purposes.** This is an example draft, not any organization's real policy and not legal advice. A real policy should be reviewed by legal, security, and HR teams.

**Version:** 0.1 draft  **Owner:** AI/Data Governance Lead  **Review cycle:** every 6 months

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets simple rules for using generative AI tools (such as chat assistants and automation platforms) at work. It applies to all employees, contractors, and interns.

## 2. Approved tools
- Only tools on the **Approved AI Tools list** may be used with company information.
- To request a new tool, submit it to the AI/Data Governance Lead. The review checks data handling, security, cost, and business need.
- Personal accounts on consumer AI tools must not be used for company data.

## 3. Data that must never go into an AI tool
Unless the tool is approved specifically for that data class:
- Personal data of customers or staff (contact details, ID numbers, health or financial details)
- Passwords, API keys, and access credentials
- Confidential client information and contract terms
- Unreleased financial results or strategy documents
- Source code or documents marked "Confidential" or higher

When in doubt, remove or replace the sensitive parts with placeholders, or ask first.

## 4. Responsible use
- **Human review:** A person must review AI output before it is shared externally or used in a decision.
- **Accuracy:** Users must verify facts, figures, and sources.
- **Accountability:** The person who uses AI output is responsible for it.
- **Fairness:** AI must not be the sole basis for decisions about hiring, pay, discipline, or credit.
- **Disclosure:** Disclose AI assistance where a client, regulator, or team rule requires it.

## 5. Approvals

| Use case | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Everyday drafting, summarizing, brainstorming with non-sensitive data | No approval needed (approved tools only) |
| New AI tool or plug-in | AI/Data Governance Lead and IT Security |
| Using personal or confidential data with AI | Data Protection Officer and department head |
| Automations that act without human review | AI/Data Governance Lead |

## 6. Incidents
If sensitive data is entered into an unapproved tool by mistake, report it to the AI/Data Governance Lead or IT Security **immediately**. Reporting early is expected and will be treated as responsible behavior.

## 7. Training and review
New staff complete a short AI safety briefing. This policy is reviewed every six months and updated as tools and laws change.
